# Open Frontier and Omnimodal EOT

Status: constitutive-reading architecture, measured on PR #18, 2026-08-22.

This specification extends `READING-POLICY.md` and `reading/READING-REASONING-ARCHITECTURE.md`. It records two corrections earned by implementation and held-out tests:

1. the Fold must contain a frontier of open structure, not only what the reader currently believes;
2. EOT/reasoning may not inherit the grammar of one language or medium.

Where this document conflicts with the Constitution, `READING-POLICY.md`, `SEED.md`, or the canonical operator algebra, those authorities win.

---

## 1. Four temporal quantities are different things

A reader encounters ordered experience. Four dynamics must not be collapsed into one score.

### Surprise

**How much did the interpreted structure change at this event?**

Surprise is instantaneous reorganization of the Fold. New structure, withdrawn structure, changed identity standing, relation recanonicalization, boundary change, frame re-zero, and frontier opening/closure may contribute.

Persistence alone does not.

A carried obligation becoming one event older is not a new surprise.

### Tension

**How much unresolved structural pressure is the current Fold carrying forward?**

Tension is a projection over open structure. It may increase while nothing surprising happens, because an unresolved obligation persists or intensifies.

### Release

**How much previously carried pressure was actually closed, satisfied, refused, reframed, superseded, merged, or otherwise transformed at this event?**

Release belongs to a prior obligation. A locally surprising event with no connection to prior open structure can have high surprise and almost no release.

### Recursion

**How much already-interpreted structure must now be re-understood because of the current event?**

A later revelation may force identity split, relation recanonicalization, testimony re-scoping, or frame change. The historical Fold at the earlier event remains immutable; the current Fold's standing toward that history changes.

## 2. The primitive is Open Structure, not “tension”

`TENSION` and `RELEASE` are not EO operators.

The underlying engine object is an unresolved structural obligation carried by the Fold.

Conceptually:

```text
OpenStructure {
  id
  terrain
  kind
  subject
  standing
  expectation?
  provenance[]
  pressure?
  openedAt
  age
  status
}
```

Examples include:

- a live identity alternative;
- an unresolved or contested proposition;
- a dependency whose consequence remains unknown;
- a scope whose boundary has not yet been earned;
- an expected continuation that has been broken;
- a conspicuous absence inside an already-earned space of possibility;
- an unexplained regime transition in a numeric field;
- an unresolved topology change;
- an interpretive hypothesis generated by the reasoner itself.

The source need not literally ask a question. Reasoning can open an obligation by making a defeasible interpretation.

## 3. Frontier is Fold state

The current Fold is not only a graph of what the reader thinks exists.

At minimum it now contains:

```text
Fold(t) {
  world
  ontology
  relations
  identityAlternatives
  unresolved
  frontier
  tension
  release
  cursor
}
```

The frontier is the current set of open structures plus an append-only transition ledger:

```text
FrontierDelta(t) {
  opened[]
  persisted[]
  resolved[]
}
```

Future forms should add explicit `intensified`, `weakened`, `split`, `merged`, `reframed`, and `superseded` transitions rather than encoding all of them as generic persistence or resolution.

The scalar tension/release traces are navigation aids downstream of this inspectable ledger.

## 4. Open structure is generated above perception

The frontier never parses text, audio, tables, pixels, or code.

Its input is already-earned unresolved structure.

Automatic sources currently include:

- unresolved identity alternatives from recursive ontology;
- unresolved/contested relations from adversarial resolution;
- generic modality obligations emitted by an emergence organ.

This is the critical separation:

```text
medium/language-specific perception
        ↓
structural observations
        ↓
modality-neutral ontology / EOT / reasoning
        ↓
open obligations
        ↓
frontier dynamics
```

Therefore no English question word, narrative-goal vocabulary, cadence label, or domain-specific emotion score is required for tension/release to exist.

## 5. Omnimodality is the stronger universality test

A mechanism that works in many languages but requires linguistic structure is still not universal.

The stronger criterion is:

> If a temporal primitive cannot operate when there is no language channel at all, it may not call itself universal.

The repository's existing perceiver architecture already implies this rule.

The audio perceiver maps PCM into field vectors and explicitly leaves onset/beat/segmentation/structure to emergence. The field perceiver requires its axis to be received rather than confusing reader scan order with material order. `loops/surf` freezes anticipation before arrival for any scalar experiential series.

The frontier therefore consumes temporal structural consequences of those organs rather than adding a special “music tension” system.

## 6. Actual audio path

The tested audio path is:

```text
PCM
 ↓
audio Reading@1
  chroma + timbre + spectral moments
 ↓
measured field channel
 ↓
causal Surf horizon
  anticipation frozen before arrival
  outcome = met | broke | flat
 ↓
continuation-break obligation
 ↓
open frontier
 ↓
tension / release trajectory
```

A `broke` horizon means the learned continuation cannot contain the arriving value.

The first break opens one integration episode.

Further `broke` or `flat` observations carry the same episode; they do not mint one obligation per frame.

The first later `met` closes that episode because the causal ground can again place experience.

This deliberately makes a weaker claim than “harmonic resolution” or “leitmotif return.” Those richer interpretations require additional independent witnesses or received musical priors.

## 7. Real Wagner result

A separate external evaluation uses a public-domain recording of Wagner material (`Die Walküre` fantasia, U.S. Marine Band, Wikimedia Commons/Musopen), with no transcript, libretto, score, chord labels, leitmotif labels, or language prior.

The first 90 seconds are decoded to mono PCM and passed through the repository's real audio perceiver.

Measured after episode correction:

```text
sample rate: 22050 Hz
excerpt: ~90 s
audio frames: 1934
Surf horizon positions: 472
met: 40
broke: 74
flat: 358

open break episodes: 8
resolved episodes: 8
final open episodes: 0
```

Two strongest release regions remained near the same places found before episode correction:

- approximately 20.62 s;
- approximately 51.64 s.

Before the episode correction the adapter falsely represented consecutive broken frames as 74 separate obligations, producing 23–24 simultaneous pseudo-tensions at those peaks. After correction, a sustained departure is one carried open structure (`open = 1`) whose pressure grows with persistence and is released once.

This establishes **modality-neutral structural tension/release**, not full understanding of Wagnerian dramatic, harmonic, or leitmotivic semantics.

## 8. EOT must not mean SVO

A separate held-out test exposed that the first recursive reader had accidentally made English word order part of ontology.

False structures included examples of the form:

```text
river may_corefer_with Nora
inspected may_corefer_with Nora
pump may_corefer_with Nora
Nora — inspected → "the"
```

The temporal architecture was better than the legacy reader, but these structures were not meaningful reading.

The correction is architectural:

### Perceiver responsibility

A language/medium-specific perceiver may use whatever evidence that medium genuinely supplies.

Examples:

```text
English:
  actor / undergoer inferred from an English structural adapter

Basque:
  roles may be supported by morphology/case/order specific to Basque

Audio:
  antecedent / continuation / recurrence / regime

Table:
  variable / value / row-scope / column-scope

Video:
  figure / motion / target / scene-boundary
```

### EOT responsibility

The universal relation shape is role-based:

```text
EORoleRelation {
  relation
  participants[] {
    role
    value
    standing
    witness
  }
  polarity
  scope
  provenance
}
```

`subject` and `object` remain temporary compatibility endpoint names inside the legacy reasoning adapter. They do **not** mean grammatical subject and object in EOT metaphysics.

## 9. English is now an adapter, not a default ontology

`perceiver/text/structural-observations.js` contains the currently implemented English structural adapter.

If text language is undeclared or unsupported, the reader may still perceive forms, but the structural adapter emits an explicit gap rather than pretending English grammar applies.

Current English evidence includes:

- tightly bounded appositional identity evidence;
- relation candidates anchored to perceived names;
- role output as `actor` / `undergoer`;
- received POS evidence to refuse nonverb-dominant connectors.

Punctuation remains evidence: tokenization may not erase a comma and thereby turn `the river station, Nora` into a false apposition.

The received English POS prior is `POSPrior@1` built from Universal Dependencies English EWT. Its giver is recorded on the reading trajectory.

This fixed a held-out error where:

```text
Nora again said ...
```

had incorrectly produced:

```text
Nora — again → said ...
```

No hardcoded “adverb blacklist” was added. The existing POS witness mechanism refused `again` as non-verb-dominant.

A held-out river-station passage is run through both the unchanged legacy whole-document reader and the constitutive reader.

Legacy result:

The legacy assembly nevertheless reports its stages as `ok`, illustrating why assembly status alone is not evidence of meaningful reading.

```text

first-event beings: 0
final beings: 1
fabricated identity alternatives: 0
provisional relations by event: [2, 3, 4, 5]
reorganization by event: [3, 1, 2, 1]
```

Grounded role relations include:

```text

Nora — said → pump worked
Nora — returned → to the station
Elias — said → pump failed
Elias — opened → sensor box
```

Earlier versions produced reorganization magnitudes around `[35, 27, 34, 43]` because every lexical n-gram perceived by Surf was being counted as provisional ontology. The current adapter keeps generic lexical forms in Surf but only promotes referent-shaped forms or explicit identity/relation participants into interpreted structure. Surprise therefore moved toward changes in the world-model rather than token churn.

The held-out test permanently refuses:

- ordinary adjacency becoming identity;
- a bare determiner becoming a relation object;
- the nonverb connector `again` becoming a relation;
- loss of role participants;
- non-monotonic causal horizon coordinates.

## 11. Recursive reasoning creates its own tension

The Rowan/courier golden proves that frontier pressure need not be stated by the source as a question.

The reader opens a live identity hypothesis:

```text
courier = Rowan ?
```

That hypothesis enters the frontier as an open Entity obligation.

While evidence remains unresolved, its age increases and tension rises.

When Rowan and the courier are experienced simultaneously as incompatible figures, recursive reasoning applies:

```text
SEG
DEF
```

The identity obligation closes, release becomes nonzero, and relations dependent on the old identity standing are re-canonicalized before the new Fold commits.

This is the desired recursive loop:

```text
interpretation
  → obligation
  → future evidence
  → attack
  → ontological transformation
  → recanonicalization
  → release / new obligations
```

## 12. Absence and NUL

Absence does not automatically generate tension.

An absence becomes an open structure only after the reading has earned a space in which presence was possible or expected.

Therefore:

```text
owner(gun, ?)
```

is not meaningful merely because a gun exists.

If the reading establishes that ownership is consequential, the unresolved role can become an open obligation. The missing value then gains structural force relative to that earned space.

This preserves the EO distinction between a universal nothing and a defined Void/Ground in which presence or absence can become coherent.

## 13. Current limitations

The following are not yet proven:

1. **Full musical semantics.** The Wagner test demonstrates structural expectation/break/integration from audio fields, not harmonic-function, cadence, leitmotif, orchestration, or dramatic-semantic interpretation.
2. **Plural audio witnesses.** RMS is currently one tested field ride. Chroma, timbre, spectral shape, boundary reorganization, and motif recurrence should become independent witnesses. Following `surf-structural.js`, they should not be fused with an arbitrary weighted score.
3. **General text-language adapters.** English is the first explicit structural adapter. Other languages should receive their own evidence paths or use stronger modality-independent structure. They must not inherit English SVO by default.
4. **Generic event adapter.** The canonical host `advanceReading()` still accepts text events at its outer boundary. The internal role-EOT, recursive ontology, frontier, and Surf-based temporal mechanics are omnimodal, but the next host refactor should let modality adapters feed one common transition contract.
5. **Richer frontier acts.** Current ledger primarily distinguishes opened/persisted/resolved. Intensify, weaken, split, merge, reframe, and supersede should become explicit typed frontier transitions.
6. **Tension pressure model.** Persistence-weighted pressure is intentionally simple. Future pressure should be derived from structural consequence, dependency, expectation strength, and perhaps competing-resolution entropy, with each source inspectable rather than fused opaquely.

## 14. Stronger definition of reading

A reader is not proven by extracting a cast, a final graph, or even a set of correct propositions.

A stronger criterion is:

> Can the reader encounter ordered material, form provisional structure without future leakage, generate legitimate unresolved obligations, carry those obligations while activation changes, identify later events that satisfy/refuse/reframe them, recursively revise the ontology and relations they depended on, and preserve the history of what it was entitled to believe at each prior moment?

Under that criterion, reading is:

> the continuous construction, carrying, resolution, and revision of open structure across ordered experience.

The current PR now has executable evidence for this in both prose reasoning and nonlinguistic audio.
