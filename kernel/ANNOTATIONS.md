## gap-002

eoreader6.1 · kernel · turn 002 — REFUSED, not earned
Attempted act: NUL*  (Void aimed at Pattern)   Stance: Unraveling   Site: Kind

The one operation, third use: PATTERN — not "this slot is empty" but
"emptiness recurring here has become a rule for what this ground does
next." Per Decal Notation's own composition table, `*` is
self-application: "the operator encountering its own pattern," not a
fresh reading of a fresh target. That is exactly why this attempt is
refused rather than recorded as earned.

Why it fails, precisely: a single crossing never oscillates — one
figure (turn 1) is just a mark, not a pattern. Only a RE-ENTRANT mark
— one that feeds its own prior output back in as material for the next
reading — can oscillate rather than settle (Spencer-Brown's re-entrant
forms; this is the formal reason Thirdness cannot reduce to
Secondness). This attempt tried to re-enter on turn 1 alone: exactly
one occurrence of the target absence, no second occurrence to test
recurrence against. Claiming a pattern here would be claiming a rule
from a sample of one — SEED.md's own name for this failure, ported
rather than reinvented, is confabulation: "it speaks without witness."

The gap, stated as a result rather than hidden as a non-event: pattern
requires a witnessed difference — a figure whose recurrence measurably
changes what the next ground looks like. With n=1, "recurring" is not
yet a fact this kernel has any standing to assert. This is refused by
type, not by degree — no amount of confidence rescues a claim that has
not yet had a second chance to fail.

What would earn it: a second, independent occurrence of absence at a
structurally comparable target (not the same target reasserted — that
would be turn 1 restated, not a recurrence), plus a measurable shift in
the ground's aperture between before-turn-1 and after-both-occurrences.
Until both exist, NUL* at this address stays a gap.

## run.mjs

The working kernel entry point. `classify(series, index)`: given material
and a candidate index, classifies it absent, or present-and-{anomalous,
ordinary}. `compare(seriesA, seriesB)`: is series A's own burstiness
"above", "below", or "peer" relative to series B's ground? Fully
calibrated, via eoreader6's real `ground()` (registered statistic, so
`reZero()`'s internal reconstruction works) and real `level()` (reseeding
null, mean+3*std over `reseeds` draws) — not the resolution-floor-only
path. Both use eoreader6's real `PERTURBATIONS`/`difference`/`admissible`,
live, never reimplemented.

## reader.mjs

A sequential reader: processes a stream one window at a time (matching
what the ground's statistic actually measures — windowMean, not a raw
value — the units mismatch caught during development), keeping a live
ground, re-zeroing on surfeit. eoreader6's real `ground()`/`difference()`/
`reZero()`, used live throughout. `read(value)` feeds one raw value and
returns null until a full window has accumulated, then a verdict.

## native.mjs

A self-contained reimplementation of `eoreader6/nul/index.js`'s entire
public API — no cross-repo import. Every function, including the seeded
RNG perturbations (`shuffle`/`resample`/`phase`), verified bit-for-bit
identical to the real module on real data (`kernel/evidence/
verify-native.mjs`, 37/37 checks). This is the actual swap-in unlock:
`run.mjs`/`reader.mjs` import from here now, not from `eoreader6` — the
kernel no longer depends on that repository being present to run. See
`PARITY.md` for what this closes and what's still open (re-earning
`kernel.eot`'s 47 turns against this calibrated mechanism; the wider
engine beyond `nul/index.js`).
