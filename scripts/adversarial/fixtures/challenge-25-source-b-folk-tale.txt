Down by the winter fires, the old women still tell of the one they call the Iron Admiral, who came from nowhere and ruled the northern sea for a single, terrible decade. They say the Iron Admiral forged the Long Chain treaty, binding six quarreling coastal clans into one fleet where a hundred years of councils had failed. Before him, so the story goes, the Vested Council took whatever toll it liked from every fishing boat that passed the strait.

The Iron Admiral sailed into the Council's own harbor with three warships and refused to leave until the tolls were struck from the ledgers. It is said the Vested Council never forgave the humiliation, though it dared not act against him while his fleet still answered to his name. Old Mother Yevni, who claims to have heard it from her grandmother, tells it differently every winter, but the ending never changes.

In every version, the Iron Admiral's ship broke apart on the Coriveil shoals in a storm that the fishermen still name after him. No wreckage was ever found on the beaches, which is why the children are told he simply sailed on, past the edge of the charted sea. Some nights, when the wind turns hard from the north, Mother Yevni swears you can still hear the Iron Admiral's bell tolling out past Coriveil.

The Vested Council, for its part, struck every mention of the treaty's author from its own records within a year of the wreck.
