Now Telemachus caused it to be declared before the assembly, and the herald set it down in the order of the house: the suitors shall vacate the hall before the rising of the sun, and shall make no further claim upon the house of Ulysses; this right is granted without warranty, and the house disclaims all liability for its exercise; notice of the aforesaid matter shall be delivered to Penelope in writing, and witnessed by the elders, in accordance with the custom of the house; and the oath here sworn shall bind the suitors and their heirs alike, in perpetuity, notwithstanding any custom of Ithaca to the contrary.
