NAVAL ARCHIVE OF THRELL SERVICE RECORD 7734

In the third year of the northern campaign, Marcus Aurelius Kade took command of the Ashgate fleet after the previous admiral fell ill. Under his direction, the fleet secured the strait against three separate incursions from the eastern raiders. Records from the flagship's log describe Admiral Kade as exacting, distant, and rarely present at shore functions. The Battle of Coriveil, fought in the eleventh month of the eleventh year, ended the northern campaign decisively.

During that engagement, Marcus Aurelius Kade ordered the third fleet into the shoals against the harbor pilot's explicit warning. His flagship, the Ardent Reach, struck the reef within the hour and was lost with most of her crew. Captain Selvi Odrun, second officer of the Ardent Reach, survived the wreck and later filed this deposition. According to Odrun's account, Kade remained on the bridge until the final signal was given to abandon ship.

No body was recovered, and the Archive lists his status as lost at sea rather than deceased. He was born on the northern coast of Threll, in the fishing town of Marrow's End, some forty years before the wreck. Before his naval service, Marcus Aurelius Kade apprenticed as a cartographer under the elder Odrun family. The Archive's clerks noted that Kade's charts of the Coriveil shoals were, ironically, the most accurate ever filed before his own ship was lost on them.

This record closes the service file of Marcus Aurelius Kade, formally entered as unresolved by order of the Ashgate command.
